# Sensor maintenance

Pressure transmitters are calibrated every six months against a reference gauge. Temperature probes are calibrated annually. A sensor that drifts more than 2% from the reference between two calibrations is replaced rather than recalibrated.

Vibration sensors mounted on rotating equipment are inspected quarterly. Loose mounting is the most common cause of false readings; a sensor that reports sudden broadband noise is almost always mechanically loose rather than detecting a real fault.

Every calibration is logged with the date, the reference instrument serial number, and the measured offset before adjustment.
