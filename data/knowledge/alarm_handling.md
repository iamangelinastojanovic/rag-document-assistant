# Alarm handling

An alarm requires acknowledgement within 15 minutes. If it is not acknowledged, it escalates to the shift supervisor.

Alarms are ranked by consequence, not by how far a value has moved. A small deviation on a safety-critical line outranks a large deviation on a non-critical one.

Any alarm that fires more than twenty times in a week is reviewed. Repeated nuisance alarms are the main reason operators begin to ignore the alarm system, so a persistently noisy alarm is treated as a defect in the alarm itself rather than as useful information.

Alarms are never disabled to silence them. The threshold is re-derived from recent normal operation and the change is documented.
